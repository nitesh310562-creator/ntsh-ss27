01The Brief

What SS27 Has To Do

Three stories, sourced from the mood board
structure and pricing proposed

Two things are true about a boy aged 2–8 in 2027, and a range has to answer both. He spends 3 hours 38 minutes a day on a screen — roughly two-thirds of it inside Roblox, Minecraft and YouTube — and he still outgrows a pair of trousers in five months. Any strategy that only serves the first is a gimmick. Any strategy that only serves the second is what everyone already sells.

SS27 is built to do both. The mood board gives us three strong, distinct stories. What this document adds is the architecture underneath them — how many options, at what prices, delivered when, and what genuinely new thing each story is carrying. Every product, colour, fabric and licence shown here is read off the SS27 mood board. Every option count, price and initiative is a proposal, and is marked as one.

Motorsport has gone mainstreamF1 fandom in India went from 60M in 2024 to 78.8M in 2026, and half of those fans arrived in the last four years. A racing story is no longer a niche licence play — it is the single most culturally live graphic territory available to boyswear right now. Road Trip is the drop that monetises it.
The screen is not the enemyAges 5–8 average 3h 38m a day, and Roblox, Minecraft and YouTube take about 65% of it. Fighting that is pointless. Outgrown is a market, not a lossKids clothing resale is $7.86B in 2026 and compounding at 12.1% — faster than kidswear overall, and in the US secondhand grew 5× faster than traditional retail. A brand that owns the second life of its own garment owns the customer twice. That is Tide Loop.

04Price Architecture

Sharpen The Entry, Push The Premium

7 tiers · ₹249 → ₹1,499
tagged PROPOSED

The AW26 ladder ran ₹299 to ₹799 in five steps and was heaviest in the middle. SS27 does the opposite of the obvious move: it drops the opening price to ₹249 to defend the doorway against quick-commerce, thins the ₹599–699 band where ranges get vague, and adds two tiers above ₹899 to carry the innovation. The result is a wider ladder, a clearer trade-up story on the hanger, and a higher average selling price despite the lower entry point.

Pit Crew Numbers

An in-store personalisation bar. Buy any Road Trip racing tee and choose a number from 1 to 99, heat-pressed on the back while you wait, plus the child's name in the racing typeface. It adds zero SKUs — the blanks are already in the range — but converts a ₹349 tee into a ₹549 ticket and gives parents a reason to come into the store rather than order online.

+₹200uplift per personalised tee
0new SKUs required
~4 minapplication time per garment
Watch outNeeds one trained associate and a heat press per flagship door. Pilot in 25 doors before national rollout.

One Blank, Many Races

Take the wins and remove the risk. Collapse the racing tee offer onto a single graded blank so the number programme extends to co-ords and caps without new patterns. Add a second licence wave and standardise the chequer, flag and number library into a reusable asset kit.

MOONSHOT — 2029/30

Garage OS

Every Road Trip garment carries a woven QR that unlocks a persistent digital garage in a Teamspirit web app. Each piece owned drops a car part — engine, spoiler, livery — and the child builds and races a virtual car against friends. Physical purchase becomes game progression, and the wardrobe becomes a collection with a visible completion state.

Grow-With-Me Coast

Solve the one problem every parent of a 2–8 has: the child grows out of it before it wears out. A hidden roll-and-button cuff on the pull-on trousers and denim shorts gives a full extra size of wear, and a UPF 40+ finish on the woven camp shirts adds a claim the parent will actually pay for. Both are cheap to add and impossible to ignore on a swing ticket.

+1 sizeextra wear per garment
UPF 40+on woven shirting
+₹45estimated cost to add, per unit
Watch outUPF claims must be lab-substantiated per fabric and colour before any ticket copy goes to print.

Roll It Everywhere

Roll the adjustable cuff across every bottom in the range, not just the two pilot blocks. Add a six-style natural-dye capsule against the WGSN #NaturalDyes cue, and standardise the stripe library so the same yarn-dye programme serves co-ords, shirting and towelling.

MOONSHOT — 2029/30

Tide Loop

A formal take-back and resale loop. Outgrown Pacific Patrol pieces come back to store, earn instant credit against the next size, and are cleaned, graded and resold from a dedicated bay. The woven ID label carries a QR holding size, wash and ownership history — so a second-hand garment arrives with a provenance record instead of a question mark.

$7.86Bkids clothing resale market, 2026
12.1%resale CAGR to 2030
5×secondhand growth vs traditional retail
Watch outHygiene, grading standards and margin on refurbishment. Run it as a credit scheme first; only take the resale margin once volume justifies it.

06The System

One Spine, Three Lanes

Product · Digital · Retail
tagged PROPOSED

The nine initiatives above are not nine projects. They run on three shared lanes, and the whole thing hangs off one decision: a QR code on every woven label. That single spine carries the collectible badges, the Garage OS, the Flavour Lab and the resale provenance record — build it once in SS27 and every later idea becomes an app update rather than a supply-chain change.

PRODUCT
  • Grow-With-Me cuffHidden roll-and-button hem on every bottom — one extra size of wear.
  • UPF 40+ shirtingLab-substantiated sun protection on woven camp shirts.
  • Modular co-ordsTops and bottoms specced to mix across all three stories.
  • Natural dye capsuleSix styles, batch variation sold as the point.
  • Non-PVC everywhereWhole range compliant, no exceptions, no caveats.
DIGITAL
  • QR on every woven labelOne scan, no sign-up, no personal data captured.
  • Collectible badgesOwning pieces unlocks a visible set — completion drives repeat.
  • WhatsApp drop alerts48 hours' notice, opt-in, the channel parents actually read.
  • AR graphic previewSee the print on the child before the changing room.
  • Garage OS / Flavour LabThe two moonshots, both built on the same QR spine.
RETAIL
  • Personalisation barHeat press, numbers and names, four minutes, +₹200 a ticket.
  • Resale bayTake-back credit scheme, graded stock, provenance on the label.
  • Drop calendar wallThe season's six micro-drops, printed and visible in store.
  • Play-test zoneLet them climb on it. Durability is the claim — prove it.
  • One-page flavour standardThe colour system, published, so merchandising holds it.

Sequencingwhat has to be true, in order

STEP 01 · NOWSpecify the QR woven label into every SS27 style. It costs almost nothing and it is the only thing that is hard to retrofit.
STEP 02 · DROP 01Pilot the personalisation bar in 25 doors with Road Trip. Measure queue time and attach rate before committing further.
STEP 03 · DROP 02Land Grow-With-Me and UPF on Pacific Patrol. These are the claims that justify the ₹1,199 tier existing at all.
STEP 04 · DROP 03Run six Snack Pack micro-drops. Carry the top three graphics into SS28 core and kill the rest without sentiment.
STEP 05 · SS28Consolidate. One blank per story, adjustable cuffs everywhere, the flavour colour standard published.
STEP 06 · 2029/30Switch on the moonshots. By then the QR spine is in three seasons of garments already in the market.
07Risk

What Could Go Wrong

5 watchouts
read before sign-off

A strategy document that only lists upside is a pitch, not a plan. These are the five things most likely to break this range, and what to do about each.

Licence concentration

Road Trip leans hard on Disney Cars, Hot Wheels and Back to the Future. If one approval slips the drop loses its spine — hold two in-house racing graphics in reserve as direct substitutes.

Drop fatigue

Six micro-drops is the ceiling, not a target. Past that, urgency reads as chaos and the core range gets cannibalised.

Personalisation ops load

A heat press is easy; a queue on a Saturday is not. Pilot 25 doors, measure the queue, and cap daily slots before scaling.

Entry price margin

₹249 defends the doorway but dilutes the mix. It only works if the ₹899+ tiers actually grow to 32% of the buy as modelled.

Colour saturation

Twenty-five shades across three stories is a lot of yarn. Enforce the seven shared shades across stories or the fabric commitment balloons.
